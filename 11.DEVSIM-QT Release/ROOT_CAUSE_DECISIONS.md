# Root-cause decisions

1. **Baseline:** fetched commit `28e5a26c16ceedeb88602a0216a478e979fea247`; all 104 STEP10 Python files match the local baseline. No tracked STEP01–10 source was edited. Independent STEP11 directory created.
2. **Flat contact rejected:** at 20×15 nm and 1e19 cm^-3, full-basis transverse SP changes density by 33.93% and neutral μ by 8.094 meV relative to the constant-profile lead; SP potential range is 74.50 mV. Keep hard-wall EMA and zero-normal-field exposed S/D electrostatics, and use this reference on both sides of the coupling contract.
3. **Boundary matching not presumed:** nonconstant nodal Dirichlet data work and preserve stiffness, but cell-averaged end profiles differ by about 7 mV at the original coarse mesh. This remains a finite-boundary/mesh issue requiring convergence, not a reason to call a different physical S/D length a numerical test.
4. **Bound states not guessed:** two equilibrium Delta_x poles are strongly localized in S/D. Their energies stabilize under frozen mode refinement, but grid/contact convergence is not complete. Classify physical origin INCONCLUSIVE. No arbitrary biased population, eta-filling claim, phonon probe or Newton-history rule added. Embedded-dark-state coverage remains incomplete.
5. **Separate mode and μ changes:** full lead μ is fixed during device projection experiments. Lead basis/DOS and lead-grid studies are separate. Fully-SCF 8/32 and 16/64 calculations use the same full lead reference. A proposed basis is not an accuracy certificate.
6. **Production storage:** use block H, banded spectral guidance and fixed-basis kinetic caches. Preserve all retained physical couplings. Dense H remains explicit for MS/Spectral and small references.
7. **Measured memory defect:** the first high-mode real-energy attempt approached the 8 GiB cap. Reducing energy-node storage alone still retained large per-panel density vectors. Replaced it with constant-memory globally budgeted adaptive accumulation. A conservative per-panel allocation prototype over-refined small contributions and was superseded after independent regression. Banded search now uses the tx embedding-shift bound and reserves multiscale panels for sub-node-width peaks, while retaining closed and shifted centres. Resource stops and failed attempts are retained; no arbitrary 180-second timeout was used.
8. **Contracts:** neutral domain exceptions, accurate unsupported-observable metadata, strict restart provenance, an environment/refinement/export/certificate CLI. Source changes in reporting scripts are separated from production changes in the provenance assessment. A uniform equilibrium migration was rejected at relative density difference 1.54e-5; it was not silently relabelled, and a fresh SCF was run.
9. **Actual biased pole diagnostic:** at the original raised-device initial VG=0.4, VD=0.01 trial, a sampled Delta_x real-part pole at 0.0274755853 eV has Gamma expectation 4.65e-34 eV, Gamma-vector norm 5.79e-18 eV, residual 9.82e-11 eV and 99.884% source localization. It lies above the global lead minimum. This is a narrow/dark candidate, not proof of an exact isolated physical state; finite numerical resolution cannot distinguish extremely weak coupling from zero. This independently demonstrates why the below-global-edge audit cannot certify this device. The sampled search is not exhaustive and no population was assigned. See `results/embedded_candidates/summary.json`.
10. **Release decision:** mandatory criteria are conjunctive. Kernel success, an equilibrium state, or a few coarse-basis current points cannot substitute for full raised-device sweeps and representative accuracy evidence. Missing criteria force Release Candidate and Teacher-ready=false.

Physical context: [Stefanucci, PRB 75, 195115 (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.75.195115) explains why bound-state populations/time dependence cannot generally be inferred from reservoir Fermi functions alone. This is not proof that these particular projected device poles are physical. The retained contour representation follows [Ozaki, PRB 75, 035123 (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.75.035123).

Final biased attempts were explicitly stopped after the actual-trial narrow/dark candidate audit and failed numerical refinement gates, not at an arbitrary walltime limit. The final raised attempt had run at least 1390.8 s (peak observed 330.6 MiB), and uniform at least 1243.2 s (255.3 MiB); neither yielded an accepted biased SCF iterate. They are INCONCLUSIVE, not a physical nonexistence proof or a successful I–V result. Equilibrium checkpoints and stage progress are retained. Full gate/drain sweeps remain NOT_RUN.
